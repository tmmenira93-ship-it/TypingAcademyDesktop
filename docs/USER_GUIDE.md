# Typing Academy Desktop - User Guide

Typing Academy is an offline desktop tutor for touch typing. You do not need an account or the internet.

## Install and open

1. Install the app for your system (Windows installer, macOS app, or Linux bundle).
2. Open Typing Academy.
3. Press Start Learning, Enter, or Space.

## Learn

1. The home page shows your level, last lesson, and a short progress summary.
2. Open Lessons to see the stages (Home Row, Top Row, Bottom Row, sentences, Shift, patterns, numbers, symbols, advanced).
3. A locked lesson opens after you finish the previous one. Each new key pair starts with a score-free introduction: you must press the right key to continue.
4. Type the shown text on your real keyboard.
5. Green letters are correct. Red letters are mistakes. Backspace corrects the last character (it can be switched off in Settings > Practice options).
6. Pause, resume, retry, or finish at any time.
7. After a lesson you see WPM, accuracy, errors, time, stars (0-5) and your result next to the lesson requirements.
8. Once the app has seen enough of your mistakes, a Weak keys practice card appears on the lesson map.

## On-screen keyboard

Keys are coloured by finger. The next key is outlined, the opposite Shift key lights up for capitals and symbols, and the finger name is shown under the text. Look at the screen, not at your hands. The keyboard and the live speed/accuracy can be hidden in Settings.

## Hands, sounds, games

The hands guide fills in the finger to use (it appears when the window is tall enough). Key sounds are off by default (Settings). After a lesson you can watch a replay of your attempt. On Home, Games has six games, including Magic Tiles (press D, F, J or K as the tiles fall and play a song): Word Rain, Letter Pop, Speed Race (race a ghost that types at your average speed), Word Sprint (60 seconds) and Key Hunt (find the key on the keyboard). Statistics > Recent attempts lets you replay earlier attempts. Voice-over (Settings) reads lesson instructions with the system voice. Statistics shows how accurate each key and each finger is.

## Results

- WPM uses correct characters divided by 5, then divided by minutes.
- Accuracy is correct characters divided by the full target length.
- Accuracy is more important than speed. Slow down if the text turns red.

## Data and privacy

- Progress is stored in a local SQLite database on this computer.
- Settings, Backup, Restore, and Delete training history are all local.
- The app does not send typing data to any server.

## Keyboard and language

English QWERTY and Arabic interface language are included. Lesson texts in this version are English so you can train standard keyboard positions.

## Helpful shortcuts

- Enter or Space on the splash screen starts learning.
- Escape leaves a lesson.
- Click the training area if the keyboard stops being captured.
